# Deep-Time Archival

## Purpose

Research durable long-term preservation of critical institutional, legal, technical, and historical information.

## Scope

- Constitutional records
- Pillar history
- Kernel specifications
- Audit evidence
- Legal records
- Architecture history
- Critical business records

## Requirements

- Integrity verification
- Version history
- Redundant storage
- Format durability
- Recovery testing
- Retention governance
