# Phase 17 Acceptance Criteria

## Customers

- Multi-product adoption measurable
- Onboarding repeatable
- Support scalable

## Developers

- Self-service development
- Production BaaS projects
- Marketplace publishing

## Partners

- Partner program operational
- Certification available
- Partner APIs versioned
- Commercial settlement auditable

## Ecosystem

- >= 50 active marketplace partners
- >= 100 external production developers
- Regional providers operational
- Partner security review operational
- No critical ecosystem-originated tenant isolation failure
