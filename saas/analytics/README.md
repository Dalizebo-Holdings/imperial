# Dalizebo Analytics

## Purpose

Dalizebo Analytics provides cross-product operational and commercial intelligence.

## P0 Capabilities

- Revenue dashboards
- Sales reporting
- Inventory reporting
- Payment reporting
- Customer reporting
- Branch reporting
- Product performance
- Exportable reports

## Data Sources

- Commerce
- POS
- CRM
- Payments
- Billing
- Inventory
- Events

## Rule

Analytics is a read-oriented data product and must not become the authority for transactional business state.
