# OS Maturity Model

## Level 0 — Undefined

No usable specification.

## Level 1 — Defined

Purpose and boundaries documented.

## Level 2 — Modelled

Interfaces, dependencies, data, and policies documented.

## Level 3 — Implemented

Capability exists and is testable.

## Level 4 — Integrated

Capability operates through standard platform contracts.

## Level 5 — Automated

Routine execution is automated and observable.

## Level 6 — Measured

Business and technical KPIs are tracked.

## Level 7 — Optimized

Continuous improvement is active.

## Rule

Lifecycle status and maturity score must remain aligned.
