# Cognitive, AI & Succession Constitutional Policy

## Approved Principles

Dalizebo may use:

- Institutional knowledge systems
- Decision records
- AI decision support
- Governed automation
- Leadership succession planning
- Organizational learning
- Market intelligence
- Behavioral analytics with appropriate privacy controls
- Brand and communications systems
- Internal education
- Multilingual terminology
- Long-horizon strategic planning

## Human Authority

AI may advise, summarize, simulate, recommend, retrieve knowledge, and execute explicitly authorized bounded workflows.

AI must not become an unaccountable replica or autonomous successor to an individual.

Material authority must remain governed through:

- Roles
- Delegation
- Approval
- Succession policy
- Access control
- Audit
- Emergency procedures

## Prohibited Patterns

Dalizebo must not implement systems whose purpose is:

- Manufacturing fake expertise
- Manipulating public perception through deception
- Feeding deliberate false information to competitors
- Psychological coercion
- Creating personality cult dependency
- Treating employees or customers as manipulable human APIs
- Making one internal institution the unquestionable source of truth
- Unbounded autonomous transfer of organizational authority

## Institutional Principle

Founder knowledge should become institutional capability.

Person
→ Decisions
→ Records
→ Knowledge
→ Policies
→ Systems
→ Institution

The institution must become stronger without becoming dependent on reproducing an individual personality.
