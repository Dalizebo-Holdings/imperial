# Physical Systems Constitutional Policy

## Approved Principles

Dalizebo may develop or operate:

- Energy infrastructure
- Logistics infrastructure
- Sensors
- Robotics
- Digital twins
- Industrial automation
- Environmental monitoring
- Mining and mineral-processing technology
- Water resilience systems
- Physical security systems
- Autonomous maintenance systems

## Mandatory Boundaries

Physical systems must be:

- Lawful
- Authorized
- Safety engineered
- Environmentally compliant
- Auditable
- Fail-safe
- Human-supervised where material consequences exist
- Geographically and operationally accountable

## Prohibited Patterns

Dalizebo systems must not be designed to:

- Evade regulators
- Conceal infrastructure from lawful oversight
- Masquerade as public infrastructure
- Covertly infiltrate municipal systems
- Create coercive dependency
- Track competitor assets through unlawful surveillance
- Automatically cause harmful physical action
- Circumvent land, water, mining, environmental, aviation, spectrum, safety, or planning law
